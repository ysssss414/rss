## B1 — Kline volume/amount units: OPEN

[Unit reconciliation](../artifacts/stage1_gate_b_retry1/unit_reconciliation.json) binds the attempted contract to AmazingData **1.1.6** `MarketData.query_kline(period=day)`. Canonical targets are **shares** and **CNY**. Raw units and conversion multipliers remain `null`. The three existing redacted live cases cover two exchanges and different volume/turnover magnitudes. Their amount ÷ volume values fall within each price range, but any equal rescaling of both fields preserves that ratio. Candidate multipliers 1, 10, 100, 1000 and 10000 cannot be uniquely discriminated without independent official daily values. A public API transcription's industry-index units describe a different endpoint and were not imported into this Kline contract.

**BLOCKER:** no version-specific official vendor unit declaration or exact SSE/SZSE per-security daily volume and amount values for the captured sessions. **WHAT WAS PROVEN:** field identity, SDK version and three internal arithmetic checks. **WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN:** shares versus lots and the absolute amount scale. **AUTHORITATIVE SOURCES CHECKED:** local AmazingData manual and installed SDK schema; public exchange daily source search. **WHY IT BLOCKS:** VWAP, liquidity, turnover and monetary thresholds can be scaled incorrectly. **MINIMUM_NEXT_ACTION:** obtain an AmazingData 1.1.6 `query_kline` unit statement and exchange official daily volume/amount records for 000001.SZ 2024-01-08, 300750.SZ 2024-09-27 and 600519.SH 2024-06-18 or equivalent mixed-volume sessions; match both fields independently across candidate multipliers.

## B2 — effective-dated historical status: OPEN

[Status reconciliation](../artifacts/stage1_gate_b_retry1/historical_status_reconciliation.json) separates publication from effective time, uses half-open intervals, records source identity/hash and defines correction links and conflict priority. For **600518.SH**, the official July 3 announcement says risk warning is removed **July 4**, with a 10% daily limit after removal. AmazingData `IS_ST_SEC` remains `1` on July 4 in both the first capture and one-day requery. This known conflict is resolved *for this documented event* by giving the issuer's explicit effective date precedence over the unqualified vendor historical snapshot. The vendor field's exact timing/error semantics are still unknown. A date-only notice is conservatively available no earlier than the end of its publication day. The one-event fixture does not establish an official continuous historical feed or a complete correction history.

**BLOCKER:** no machine-retrievable official historical event archive with first publication, effective dates and correction/supersession links; independent NORMAL→ST and *ST transition validations remain absent. **WHAT WAS PROVEN:** one issuer ST→NORMAL event and a real vendor conflict; deterministic selection/overlap guards. **WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN:** vendor timing semantics, archive coverage and revision chronology. **AUTHORITATIVE SOURCES CHECKED:** CNINFO issuer notice and exchange technical references. **WHY IT BLOCKS:** the current historical status field can assign the wrong trading regime on a known date. **MINIMUM_NEXT_ACTION:** obtain an exchange/CNINFO event index or daily static reference archive with first-seen and revision metadata, then reconcile at least one official NORMAL→ST, ST→NORMAL and *ST case to daily regimes, including every conflicting vendor row.

**BLOCKER:** no verified first availability and reproducible correction/version history for the minimum causal raw feeds. **WHAT WAS PROVEN:** positive official rule selection, future-publication rejection and unknown-source rejection. **WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN:** as-of semantics of historical AmazingData bars/calendar/status/limits. **AUTHORITATIVE SOURCES CHECKED:** versioned SSE rule and live SDK captures. **WHY IT BLOCKS:** a historical query may contain corrections or labels learned after the decision cutoff. **MINIMUM_NEXT_ACTION:** capture first-available timestamps, immutable payload versions and revision/supersession logs for each core feed; replay each at a historical decision cutoff and require all necessary fields before admitting any derived feature.
