## SSE contract

The [2023 trading rule](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/repeal/rules/c/c_20250612_10824490.shtml) governs the sampled pre-2026-07-06 days. The [2026 rule notice](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/stocks/exchange/c/c_20260424_10816482.shtml) makes the revised rule effective on **2026-07-06**, replacing the 2023 rule; the [exchange explanation](https://www.sse.com.cn/aboutus/mediacenter/hotandd/c/c_20260424_10816474.shtml) confirms the risk-warning daily band changes from 5% to 10%. The dated registry selects one rule by exchange, board, regime and trading date; missing or overlapping versions fail closed.

The [SSE market-data interface](https://www.sse.com.cn/services/tradingtech/development/c/10781338/files/b251f026187849cdb57db7d4e2205269.pdf) describes `cpxx0201` limit type `N` as daily limit control, with effective upper/lower prices. `R` and `U` are no-limit/no-control types; `P` is IPO first-day control and its displayed range is not automatically a daily limit. `cpxx0202` can update a first-batch field. If actual rows are later obtained, the final applicable pre-open version takes precedence; unresolved same-time conflicts invalidate the day. This qualification uses the official rule, dated events, exchange previous close, 0.01-CNY tick, half-up rounding, and the one-tick minimum to reconstruct the limits. It compares AmazingData `HIGH_LIMITED` and `LOW_LIMITED` with those independent values. The SSE ex-dividend reference is adjusted under the exchange rule rather than copied from an unadjusted yesterday close.

## SZSE contract

The [SZSE reference-file specification](https://www.szse.cn/marketServices/technicalservice/interface/P020241016554266648071.pdf) defines `cashauctionparams` `HasPriceLimit`, `ReferPriceType`, `LimitType`, rates, absolute limit prices and `PriceTick`. `HasPriceLimit=N` gives canonical null bounds; `ReferPriceType=1` selects previous close. `LimitType=1` calculates from percentage rates and the reference, rounded to tick; the [official interface FAQ](https://www.szse.cn/www/marketServices/technicalservice/introduce/P020180328467244590967.pdf) says `LimitUpAbsolute` and `LimitDownAbsolute` have **no meaning in this mode**. `LimitType=2` names actual absolute upper/lower limit prices, subject to ordering and tick checks. Unknown types invalidate the day. The representative SZSE cases use the [2023 trading rule](https://investor.szse.cn/lawrules/index/rule/t20230217_598773.html) plus official daily `qss` reference and 0.01 tick; they do not claim an observed historical `LimitType` row. [SZSE's IPO guidance](https://investor.szse.cn/index/update/t20230509_600303.html) confirms the first five trading days have no daily price limit.

## Provider admission and failure handling

For the tested SSE limited days, `HIGH_LIMITED`/`LOW_LIMITED` are qualified **only after** independent reconstruction. The 2026 boundary proves that supplier rate fields cannot override dated rules; 600518 proves `IS_ST_SEC` cannot override the official regime. The historical ST label remains auxiliary and excluded from causal features. For SZSE, the official rate/reference reconstruction is authoritative under the tested rules; AmazingData rates and bounds corroborate the cases, while its absolute field has no independent authority in `LimitType=1`. No-limit `0`/`999` sentinels normalize to null.

`DailyTradingConstraint` carries source provenance, raw supplier fields and validation status. Missing reference, unknown/overlapping historical rule, unknown mode, invalid sentinel, impossible ordering, off-tick price, reference or rounding mismatch, or unresolved supplier conflict gives `INVALID`, excludes that security-day, and emits a structured reason. `IS_CLOSE_LIMIT_UP` requires trading allowed, limit applicable and exact equality in integer ticks. `IS_10_PERCENT_REGIME` derives from the validated dated constraint, never the ST label or observed return. These are data-contract definitions, not an implemented observation pool or strategy.
