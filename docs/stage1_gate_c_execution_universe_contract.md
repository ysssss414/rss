## C1 — signal, order, execution and price are separate events

The original “收盘买，如果涨停就打板” cannot mean both **use final T close/RSI14/final close-at-limit as signal inputs** and **guarantee a fill at that same final T close**. Gate B's decision-time contract places final OHLCV and derived EOD values at or after finalization. The [SSE 2023 rule](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/repeal/rules/c/c_20250612_10824490.shtml), [SSE 2026 rule](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/stocks/exchange/c/c_20260424_10816482.shtml) and [SZSE 2023 rule](https://investor.szse.cn/lawrules/index/rule/t20230217_598773.html) establish ordinary opening auction 09:15–09:25 and closing auction 14:57–15:00; they do not prove any historical order was accepted or filled. Date-versioned exceptions must be checked for each future replay.

| Policy ID | SIGNAL_TIME | ORDER_TIME | EXECUTION_TIME / PRICE | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `NEXT_SESSION_V1` | After T final bar and every T input's `available_at`; never a date-only midnight marker. | T+1 **next exchange session from the frozen qualified calendar** at 09:15 for a modeled opening-auction marketable-limit buy. | T+1 opening auction at 09:25 if qualified; modeled price is its official **raw** open, observed after the auction. | `CONTRACT_QUALIFIED`; recommended first causal daily-data benchmark. |
| `CLOSING_AUCTION_V1` | Before T's applicable auction order cutoff, from genuinely available pre-order observations; no final T values. | During T's documented closing-auction submission window. | T auction match/price only with accepted-order, liquidity and queue evidence. | `NOT_QUALIFIED`; historical pre-order/auction/order evidence missing. |
| `PRE_CLOSE_SNAPSHOT_V1` | Frozen timestamped T snapshot before the valid order cutoff; 14:55 is a candidate, 14:57 is the auction boundary, not an assumed valid snapshot. | After that snapshot and before an evidenced submission cutoff. | T fill/price only under a separately qualified order model. | `NOT_QUALIFIED`; historical minute/quote timing and fill evidence missing. |

`NEXT_SESSION_V1` freezes the following deliberately conservative **model**, not a broker fill claim. T+1 must have a valid pre-open Gate B constraint and be trading; the frozen daily bar must show an official positive open strictly **below** the validated upper limit. Then the benchmark records `MODELLED_FILL` at that open with explicit **0 bps idealized slippage**. An opening exactly at the upper limit gives `NO_FILL`; suspension/non-trading, missing/invalid opening price, no-limit or invalid constraint gives `EXECUTION_UNRESOLVED`; no automatic rollover occurs. The open is a T+1 outcome, never a T decision input. Fees, spread, impact, capacity and actual order size remain unmodelled. A zero-slippage open below the limit is not proof of actual quantity available. This policy is a causal benchmark and **does not preserve the original same-close execution intent**.

For “涨停就打板”, distinguish a limit-up close, sealed board, opening/resealing path, available sell liquidity and queue position. `close == limit_up` never implies a same-day buy fill. With daily data alone, same-day limit-up execution is `NO_FILL` under the conservative benchmark or `EXECUTION_UNRESOLVED` under an unqualified policy. The contract does not implement a queue model or assume 100% fill. A future backtest must freeze `execution_policy_id`, signal cutoff, allowed input availability, order rule, execution rule, fill/price source, slippage, limit-up assumption, data snapshot and trading-calendar snapshot. It must not label results from different policies as the same strategy execution.

## C2 — `HistoricalUniverse(t)` is a point-in-time security-day contract

The output key is `(security_id, trade_date, execution_policy_id, data_snapshot_id, universe_contract_version)`, with `eligible` and **one structured primary exclusion reason**. It is not a current stock list. For this contract's `NEXT_SESSION_V1` cutoff, use only evidence with effective date `<= t` and `available_at <= decision_at`. The approved decision point follows T EOD finalization; T's final bar may count only after it is available. Any future intraday policy needs a separately versioned universe cutoff. A later delisting or correction cannot remove a stock from an earlier as-of universe.

Only explicitly typed `A_SHARE_COMMON_STOCK` can enter; ETF, fund, bond, convertible bond, B-share, preferred stock, REIT, other and unknown types are excluded. Type must come from dated provider/exchange evidence, **never ticker pattern**. Listing events use `LISTED / DELISTED / RELISTED`, their effective dates, availability timestamps, source identity/hash and verified as-of coverage. Conflicting or incomplete lifecycle evidence fails closed. Board (SSE Main, SZSE Main, ChiNext, STAR, BSE) is descriptive or a dated rule-lookup input; it never overrides the day's canonical constraint.

Eligibility also requires explicit `TRADING` status, a present valid T bar, a Gate B `DailyTradingConstraint` with `validation_status=VALID`, `limit_applicable=true` and exactly **10% upper and lower daily limit rates**. `HISTORY_REQUIREMENT = DEPENDENCY_DRIVEN`: `minimum_history_required` is the maximum lookback of admitted strategy primitives, supplied explicitly in the frozen run context. With no admitted primitives in this foundation, zero is permitted. The earlier 150-session requirement came from legacy `warmup_trading_days=150`, which [requests indicator warm-up only](three_board_rsi_entry_v0.1.md) and even retains audit results for newly listed securities without 150 bars. It is an optional configurable, non-semantic research default, not a fixed Stage 1 eligibility or listing-age rule. The 20 synthetic fixtures explicitly use a seven-observation scenario to preserve their boundary expectations; seven is not a production default. An IPO has no hard-coded blacklist: no-limit days or an explicitly required unmet dependency exclude it. Suspension cannot be inferred from a missing bar or zero volume. Unknown historical security type, listing coverage, status or constraint excludes the security-day, rather than guessing from current labels, board, ST name or observed percentage change.

A qualified history observation is one distinct exchange trading session since the latest `LISTED` or `RELISTED` event with explicit `TRADING` status and a valid stock EOD bar available by `decision_at`; T may count only after its bar finalizes. Calendar days, exchange sessions without a valid stock bar, and days in the eligible 10% regime are **different counts**. The latter regime is required for the candidate security-day, not for every historical bar. Missing, invalid, suspended or late bars do not count. A frozen source must substantiate the count; synthetic precomputed counts do not qualify live coverage.

Structured primary reasons include `NOT_LISTED`, `DELISTED`, `SUSPENDED`, `NO_PRICE_LIMIT`, `NON_10_PERCENT_REGIME`, `INVALID_DAILY_CONSTRAINT`, `MISSING_BAR`, `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`, `UNSUPPORTED_SECURITY_TYPE`, `UNKNOWN_LISTING_LIFECYCLE`, `UNKNOWN_TRADING_STATUS`, `NON_TRADING`, `MISSING_PIT_EVIDENCE` and `DATA_CONFLICT`. The [validator](../scripts/validate_stage1_gate_c_contract.py) fixes the check order; the same frozen snapshot, contract versions, policy cutoff, lookback and date must yield the same ordered result. Future run context must record `data_snapshot_id`, `daily_constraint_contract_version`, `universe_contract_version`, `execution_policy_id`, `decision_at` and `minimum_history_required`.

The 20 **synthetic** contract cases cover ordinary SSE/SZSE 10% eligibility; ChiNext/STAR 20%; IPO/special no-limit; historical risk-warning 5%; the post-rule-change 10% boundary; suspension; insufficient scenario history; delisting and relisting; invalid constraint; ETF type; missing bar; future delisting not rewriting past; unknown lifecycle; a not-yet-listed security; conflicting lifecycle; and late type evidence. They verify four eligible and sixteen excluded decisions under their explicit seven-observation fixture threshold. They do **not** prove a complete real-market historical universe, actual intraday availability or execution fills.
