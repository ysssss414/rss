## Contract and input semantics

For `d <= T`, the frozen price basis remains `adjusted_close(d as of T) = raw_close(d) * B(d)/B(T)`. Absolute `B` stability is **not** required. The required property is that `R(d,T)=B(d)/B(T)` is determined solely by final implemented actions effective no later than `T`. The bounded AmazingData 1.1.6 capture used `is_local=False`; the [official SDK documentation](https://amazing.ptradeapi.com/) calls `get_backward_factor` a cumulative backward factor and `get_adj_factor` a single-event factor, both indexed by trading date with security-code columns. `is_local=True` may return a stale local cache; `False` refreshes it. The docs do **not** specify decimal precision, old-snapshot immutability, or revision history. [API contract](../artifacts/stage1_factor_pit/factor_api_contract.json).

The same docs say dividend and rights `begin_date`/`end_date` filter **announcement dates**, not ex-dates. For dividends, `DATE_EX` is effective time, `DATE_EQY_RECORD` is the preceding record day, `DATE_DVD_ANN` is implementation announcement, and `IS_CHANGED` flags a revised plan. `DIV_PROGRESS=3` was observed on implemented rows with `DATE_EX`; its meaning is corroborated by issuer notices rather than assumed from an undocumented progress-code table. For rights, `EX_DIVIDEND_DATE` is effective; `EXECUTE_DATE`, `RESULT_DATE`, and `ANN_DATE` have distinct meanings. A date-only `known_at` is accepted only if strictly before the ex-date; a same-day announcement needs a verified timestamp or fails closed.

## Three evidence layers

Layer A — Eight stratified SSE/SZSE securities, 8,732 factor-calendar dates, and **100** changes since 2014-01-01: 99 implemented dividend/stock distributions and one rights issue. On every change, `B(t) = B(previous trading date) * A(t)` within the observed six-decimal `B` rounding interval; no unexplained factor-change date and no unexplained non-one single-event factor. The empirical precision rule uses `Decimal`, quantum `q=0.000001`, and the conservative ratio interval `(B(d)-q/2)/(B(T)+q/2)` through `(B(d)+q/2)/(B(T)-q/2)`. This precision is **empirical**, not SDK-documented. No default floating-point `isclose` is used. [Provider validation](../artifacts/stage1_factor_pit/provider_validation.json).

Layer B — Official company/exchange action terms reconcile to provider single-event and cumulative factors, including 600030.SH rights (2022-01-27), 001331.SZ transfer-only (2025-06-05), 688115.SH revised cash+bonus (2026-07-15), and 300059.SZ cash+transfer (2023-04-18). The previous 600519.SH 2015 bonus+cash and 2024 cash cases remain as [supporting evidence](../artifacts/stage1_real_data_event_study/factor_qualification.json). The new cases use prior raw close and issuer terms to compute a tick-rounded (CNY 0.01, half-up) ex-reference, then compare its ratio to six-decimal provider-factor bounds. [Case calculations](../artifacts/stage1_factor_pit/corporate_action_cases.json), [001331 implementation notice](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-05-28/1223695872.PDF), [600030 issuer terms](https://www.citics.com/newsite/tzzgx/ggyth/gg/aggg/202201/P020220113696056948802.pdf), [600030 success result](https://www.citics.com/newsite/news/202201/t20220127_1164678.html), [688115 implementation notice](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-07-08/688115_20260708_3QD6.pdf), [300059 annual report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-03-15/1219307793.PDF).

The observed implemented event mix is 74 cash-only, 3 cash+bonus, 20 cash+transfer, 1 cash+bonus+transfer, 1 transfer-only, and 1 rights. A standalone **bonus-only implemented** event was `NOT_PRESENT_IN_QUALIFICATION_RANGE`; the 688115 original bonus-only *proposal* was explicitly not counted as implemented. Both older and recent events, both exchanges, and securities with sequential actions are represented.

Layer C — For each `d<T`, the empirically determined orientation gives `R(d,T) = 1 / product(A(e), d<e<=T)`. The verifier truncates at `T` before comparing with today's `B_current(d)/B_current(T)`. Across 642 security/interval pairs with at least one later event, including 550 with two or more later events, none escaped the same six-decimal factor bounds. Five externally reconciled representatives include both one-later-event and multiple-later-event cases. The historical 600519 2024 cash ratio, for example, is `0.9797042375953993844766630277` from current `B` versus `0.9797042375953993577925980332` from the event-only product, with **four** later actions excluded. There was no genuine pre-action `B` export, so this is the explicitly permitted **event-decomposition** path, not a direct old/new snapshot claim. [Future-invariance cases](../artifacts/stage1_factor_pit/future_invariance_cases.json).

A common multiplicative normalization cancels from `B(d)/B(T)` and is allowed. A date-dependent normalization that changes the ratio is rejected by `FactorRatioSnapshot.ratio`; synthetic tests cover both. The cache contract stores `security_id`, `trade_date`, raw six-decimal factor, `source_snapshot_id`, source hash, and the independently qualified effective-event set. It computes and verifies ratios on demand, rather than storing bulk adjusted-close histories.

## Revisions and fail-closed rule

The current provider dividend table is a *current record*, not a versioned historical archive. A proposal, even shareholder-approved, cannot adjust prices before effective date. A changed row or multiple versions must have a final issuer implementation notice and explicit supersession evidence before admission. `CorporateAction.vendor_changed` or multiple versions without `revision_evidence` raises; a final plan first known on/after the ex-date raises; an unresolved future action is excluded from a prior `T` without poisoning that earlier ratio. This is a **date/security admission gate**, not silent acceptance of current rows.

For 688115.SH, the originally approved 2025 plan was bonus `0.4` per share, no cash. The [2026-06-13 revised proposal](https://cniis.aastocks.com/CNSESH_STOCK/2026/2026-6/2026-06-13/12391849.pdf) added CNY `0.067` cash per share but still required shareholder approval; the [2026-07-08 final implementation](https://static.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2026-07-08/688115_20260708_3QD6.pdf) took effect 2026-07-15. The provider's `IS_CHANGED=1` final terms match the latter, not the superseded bonus-only proposal. Seven other `IS_CHANGED=1` security/dates in the sample remain **UNRESOLVED_FOR_FUTURE_MATERIALIZATION** until their own issuer notices are reconciled; they must never be admitted by merely setting a Boolean. [Revision register](../artifacts/stage1_factor_pit/revision_semantics.json).

The 600030.SH rights record's `ANN_DATE=2022-02-10` is a later record update, not its first-known time. The issuer gave execution terms earlier and [announced successful A-share subscriptions at 2022-01-26 18:20](https://www.citics.com/newsite/news/202201/t20220127_1164678.html), before the 2022-01-27 ex-rights session. Treating `ANN_DATE` as the first-known time would incorrectly reject a valid event; treating a proposed rights plan as executed would incorrectly admit one.

An unannounced future correction could theoretically change a historical ratio. SDK documentation does not guarantee that cannot happen. For that reason, a future materialization must freeze a snapshot/hash, independently reconcile every effective action on the requested security prefix, and reject any later non-common revision; D5 qualification does **not** waive these checks or license today's forward-adjusted OHLCV as a fallback.
