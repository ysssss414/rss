This is useful negative control, but stock_basic has no explicit `SECURITY_TYPE`; relisting and contiguous effective-dated type/lifecycle intervals remain untested. Eligibility uses effective dates only: a known future delisting cannot exclude days before it takes effect.

The historical status endpoint and checked bars agree on three 600519.SH days around its 2024 dividend; a four-day 600518.SH status sample includes the [issuer-confirmed 2024-07-03 suspension](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-07-03/1220520500.PDF). A sampled ST-label conflict precludes using that flag as authoritative limit regime. Missing bar alone is `UNKNOWN`, not `SUSPENDED`. Gate B bar units (shares/CNY) and the DailyTradingConstraint C2 contract remain closed, but no full real bar snapshot or canonical per-day constraint totals exist.

Two [issuer company-action cases](../artifacts/stage1_real_data_event_study/factor_qualification.json) match the vendor pre/post factor ratios within the exact six-decimal display-precision bounds: the 2015 bonus-and-cash case and the [2024 cash case](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-06-12/1220324741.PDF). Two factor retrievals have 8,730 overlapping, unchanged values, yet both were made long after those actions. Thus they do **not** prove ratio invariance across a later action. No representative rights or multiple-action reconstruction, or universe-wide target-window factor coverage, is available. `PIT_ADJUSTED_CLOSE_V1` therefore remains disallowed for real signal generation. Absolute factor stability is not demanded; the required object is `factor(d)/factor(T)` for `d <= T`.

`ENTRY_COMPARABLE_FORWARD_PATH_V1` / `PRICE_PATH_V1` is recorded separately in the [outcome contract](../artifacts/stage1_real_data_event_study/corporate_action_outcome_contract.json). A realized future action may rescale future high/low/close to the raw entry-price basis for MFE, MAE and RET, while no cash dividend is credited as income. This is price-path comparability, not total shareholder return. Future actions and late factors are forbidden from HistoricalUniverse, RSI, MA, limit-up counts, ObservationPool and Entry signals. A synthetic guard test enforces that boundary; a complete real action chronology has not been qualified. The engine and its frozen horizons remain unchanged.

## Reproducibility and publication boundary

The private `.local_research_data/` area contains full calendar sessions, factor HDF5 files, source probe receipts and case inputs. It is gitignored; no full vendor rows or event-level price paths are public. Public JSON contains only source hashes, limited counts, schemas, official links and derived validation. The private calendar snapshot, factor snapshots and probes are partial receipts, **not** `REAL_RESEARCH_SNAPSHOT_V1`. A real run requires a single frozen manifest with provider/version, retrieval timestamps, request universe/range/parameters, raw and normalized hashes, row/security/date counts, contract versions and code commit for D1-D8.

The local checks are:

```text
py -3.13 -B scripts/validate_real_calendar_snapshot.py --private-snapshot .local_research_data/calendar_20260923_retry.json

```
