## C1 — AmazingData 1.1.6 `query_kline` units: CLOSED for unit conversion

The installed SDK exposes `Kline.volume` as `Optional[int]` and `amount` as `Optional[float]`; neither the compiled field metadata nor local manual page 145 states this endpoint's units. The frozen redacted SDK captures were compared **independently** with exchange-controlled daily data, with raw responses and SHA-256 recorded in the [source registry](../artifacts/stage1_gate_b_retry2/source_registry.json). The SSE day-k response matched volume exactly in shares and reported amount to whole CNY for five stock-days, including low-priced 600518, high-priced 600519, 688981, and 2026 risk-warning 600187. Five SZSE 000001 stock-days matched the exchange report's displayed **0.01 万股 / 0.01 万元** precision after dividing each raw field by 10,000 and truncating to the displayed precision. In every comparable row, the unique candidate multiplier for each raw field among 1, 10, 100, 1000, 10000 was **1**. Canonical conversions are therefore raw `volume × 1 = shares` and raw `amount × 1 = CNY`.

There is a separate source-scope anomaly: 300750.SZ on 2024-09-27 has vendor volume 70,181,326 shares and amount 15,567,947,397.43 CNY, whereas the SZSE report displays 7,019.45 万股 and 1,557,095.48 万元. The report footer includes all trading methods; the exact cause of this difference is **unproven**. This row is excluded from unit identification and flagged for content-quality investigation, not described as a unit match. The five matching SZSE dates belong to one security, so unit inference across other SZSE securities has lower breadth than SSE. C1 certifies the unit multiplier, not universal bar parity. [Reconciliation and row-level candidates](../artifacts/stage1_gate_b_retry2/market_unit_reconciliation.json).

## C2 — DailyTradingConstraint: OPEN

The canonical object contains security/date, reference price, limit-applicable state/type, absolute upper/lower prices, tick, trading/suspension state, source/priority and retrieval time. [SSE's technical specification](https://www.sse.com.cn/services/tradingtech/development/c/10781338/files/b251f026187849cdb57db7d4e2205269.pdf) describes `cpxx0201MMDD` and price-updating `cpxx0202MMDD`: the final version **before** session open takes precedence, and a displayed bound must be interpreted with its limit type. [SZSE's data-file specification](https://www.szse.cn/marketServices/technicalservice/interface/P020241016554266648071.pdf) describes `securities` and `cashauctionparams`, including status, tick, `HasPriceLimit`, reference/limit type, rate and absolute parameters. [SZSE's FAQ](https://www.szse.cn/www/marketServices/technicalservice/introduce/P020180328467244590967.pdf) gives tick rounding and real-time displayed limit prices. The final non-`pre` daily file wins over preliminary data. Historical per-security reference **rows** for the contested dates were not obtained from public official sources; the schemas alone do not prove vendor daily prices.

Priority is final exchange daily reference; then vendor `PRECLOSE/HIGH_LIMITED/LOW_LIMITED` **only after** comprehensive independent qualification; then case-specific official rule/issuer notice plus official quote corroboration; descriptive ST labels and vendor rate fields are diagnostic only. Equal-priority conflicting versions, missing/invalid limits, impossible or off-tick prices, and unresolved rule/reference conflict exclude the security-day and emit a quality flag. For a verified no-limit day, 0/999 sentinels remain in `raw_provider_value`, while the domain value is `limit_applicable=false`, upper/lower `null`. Stage 1's 10% universe must use board eligibility and a verified actual daily 10% constraint, never the stock name. A limit-up close requires the **raw close to equal the verified absolute upper limit**; touching a high or crossing a percentage threshold is insufficient. [Contract](../artifacts/stage1_gate_b_retry2/daily_trading_constraint_contract.json).

The 600518.SH conflict is split cleanly. On **2024-07-04**, AmazingData reports `PRECLOSE=1.93`, `HIGH_LIMITED=2.12`, `LOW_LIMITED=1.74`, both `PRICE_*_LMT_RATE=0.10`, `IS_ST_SEC=1`, and OHLC all `2.12`. The [issuer's effective-date notice](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-07-03/1220520500.PDF) removes risk warning July 4, after a July 3 suspension, and restores the 10% regime. The official SSE day-k row confirms OHLC all `2.12`. **The historical label is wrong.** The absolute upper/lower prices are consistent with the official 10% regime and observed close, but the exact daily exchange reference row was not acquired, so **both absolute bounds are not independently certified**. On suspended July 3, vendor absolute prices fit 5% while its rate fields say 10%; no bar exists and the day is excluded. On **2026-07-06**, a new bounded AmazingData 1.1.6 capture for 600187.SH shows `PRECLOSE=1.47`, upper/lower `1.62/1.32` (the [SSE 10% rule](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/stocks/exchange/c/c_20260424_10816482.shtml) effective that date), but vendor rate fields still say 5%. The official SSE day-k high is `1.62`; lower is not directly observed. These cases support absolute-price priority over the descriptive rate, without qualifying the full feed.

The [validation matrix](../artifacts/stage1_gate_b_retry2/daily_constraint_validation.json) records normal 10%, historical 5%, post-2026 10%, 20%, first-five-day IPO no-limit, ex-dividend, removal, suspension, near-limit and actual close-limit cases. The [SSE-hosted 603194 listing announcement](https://www.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2024-12-23/603194_20241223_YCS3.pdf) independently confirms the IPO exception. The [SSE dividend record](https://big5.sse.com.cn/aboutus/publication/yearly/documents/c/10801255/files/02acb566841a48f0b2ca8941c73f4541.pdf) supports the 600519 June 19 cash dividend of 30.876 CNY/share; 1521.50 prior close minus that dividend gives adjusted reference 1490.62 after tick rounding. The **risk-warning introduction boundary remains MISSING**. Several matrix cells have only rule/quote corroboration, not the exact exchange reference upper and lower values. Consequently the fallback qualification has **not** proven vendor absolute prices across the matrix or both exchanges. C2 remains OPEN, and vendor daily limits are not globally admitted as a core causal source.

## C3 — Event time and decision cutoff: CLOSED

`PRE_OPEN_KNOWN` data are usable after the final pre-open publication; `INTRADAY_OBSERVED` data after observation; raw `EOD_FINALIZED` OHLCV/amount only after bar finalization; `DERIVED_EOD` availability is the latest input availability. Timezone-aware cutoff checks reject T+1 bars at a T decision, T final close at 14:30, unknown publication semantics, and unfrozen raw snapshots. A T finalized bar is admitted after finalization. Production backtest acquisition must freeze retrieval timestamp, provider version, request parameters/hash and raw content hash; re-retrieval creates a new immutable version. Full first-write/revision logs for raw OHLCV are unavailable but are not necessary for this event-time contract. News, fundamentals, theme labels and manual annotations remain **PROHIBITED_FOR_CAUSAL_BACKTEST** without strict publication/revision PIT; they are outside the present core set. [Contract](../artifacts/stage1_gate_b_retry2/decision_time_contract.json) and [fixtures](../artifacts/stage1_gate_b_retry2/pit_validation.json).
