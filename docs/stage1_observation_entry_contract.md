# Stage 1 ObservationPool and entry signal contract

This branch starts from public `main` `a44c0345ca3663bf466c4f41bc80fb810a063a78`, the merge of primitive PR #11. It defines only `OBSERVATION_POOL_V1`, `ENTRY_A_PULLBACK_TO_MA5_V1`, and `ENTRY_B_RSI_RECROSS_70_V1`. All market fixtures are synthetic; no real-data strategy qualification, position, exit, PnL, full backtest, parameter sweep, or market-regime filter is claimed.

At finalized T EOD, admission requires an eligible `HistoricalUniverse(T)`, ready `LIMIT_UP_COUNT_5_V1` equal to 4 or 5, and ready `RSI14_PROJECT_V1` strictly above 70. The RSI identity is the project's first-valid-delta-seeded 14-period recurrence on `PIT_ADJUSTED_CLOSE_V1`; it needs two valid prices for its first value but must replay the complete valid prefix from the latest listing/relisting. Dependency absence, invalidity, or insufficient history produces `NO_ADMISSION_EVALUATION`, distinct from a ready false condition. The count and RSI admission threshold are **not** continuing pool eligibility requirements.

An admission creates a unique security/date/version-bound instance. Its admission exchange session is index 1; session 7 remains eligible for EOD signal generation, then expires at EOD if no signal is pending. Weekends and exchange holidays do not advance the index. A stock suspension advances the exchange-session index but produces no entry evaluation; a pure suspension does not invalidate. Delisting, unsupported security type, invalid DailyTradingConstraint, a switch away from the validated symmetric 10% limit regime, or PIT/data conflict invalidates the instance. An active or pending instance prevents overlapping admission. After expiry, invalidation, or termination after a separately modeled fill, a later date may create a new instance.

Admission day itself can generate a signal at T EOD. Entry A needs an active pool, eligible universe, current RSI strictly above 70, no earlier RSI below 70 in this pool, and ready MA5. `PIT_ADJUSTED_CLOSE_V1` is frozen at T with `adjusted_close(d)=raw_close(d)*factor(d)/factor(T)`. The T factor and anchor factor must be verified PIT inputs available by T EOD; their T ratio is exactly 1 and the adjusted T close must equal the raw T close. The contract explicitly converts `MA5_T_RAW_EQUIVALENT=MA5_adjusted/(factor(T)/anchor_factor(T))` before comparing raw prices: `raw_low(T)<=MA5_T_RAW_EQUIVALENT<=raw_close(T)`. No exact close equality or tolerance is added. Missing MA5 readiness is not a false Entry A condition.

Entry B requires that the pool has seen RSI below 70, the **previous valid evaluation** RSI is strictly below 70, current RSI is strictly above 70, and current T-anchored indicator close is higher than the previous valid evaluation's indicator close. An exactly 70 value is neither below nor above. A suspension preserves the previous valid evaluation; MA5 is not a B dependency. Signals retain a tuple of all qualifying reasons without a priority rule. Under these two frozen predicates, A and B are mutually exclusive because A forbids a previous below-70 observation and B requires it.

Each signal records instance, contract/primitive versions, snapshot, factor source hash, date, reasons, and execution intent. A ready close-limit-up event labels `LIMIT_UP_CLOSE_BOARD_INTENT`; otherwise the label is `NORMAL_CLOSE_INTENT`. The board label records original strategy intent and an `EXECUTION_FIDELITY_GAP`: qualified `NEXT_SESSION_V1` cannot reproduce a same-day T close board purchase. Both labels map to a separate next-exchange-session buy attempt. `SIGNALLED` is never a fill or position. `MODELLED_FILL` terminates this pool without modeling a position; `NO_FILL` returns it to `ACTIVE` while within the seven-session horizon; `EXECUTION_UNRESOLVED` remains pending without producing new signals, but its exchange-session age keeps advancing and an unresolved instance expires at session 7 EOD. A valid session-7 signal may execute on session 8, but session 8 cannot generate a new signal for that instance.

The [versioned contracts](../artifacts/stage1_observation_entry/observation_pool_contract.json), [state cases](../artifacts/stage1_observation_entry/state_transition_cases.json), and synthetic tests make these boundaries reviewable. Real historical use still requires an independently qualified PIT factor history and frozen universe inputs; this branch does not supply a production market feed.
